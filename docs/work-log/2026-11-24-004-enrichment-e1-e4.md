## Changes and findings so far

- **Encoder over the whole corpus** (GLiNER-Relex, GPU, 83.5 ms per paper, 13,617 papers, 19 min); outputs
  identical to the CPU run on the 100 development papers (`data/interim/enrichment-v1/mentions-relex.jsonl`,
  ignored). `ops/extract/run_encoders.py` is now resumable and flushes per chunk.
- **SLM verification and relation generation over the whole corpus** (Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M, config frozen from
  K1): about 0.4 papers per second because the laptop GPU runs at about 1.1 GHz under its power cap, so the
  run takes roughly 9 hours. Resumable by paper. A compact text format for relations halved completion
  tokens but dropped dev relation F1 from 0.400 to 0.325, so it was not used (`verify-text` mode kept as a
  documented variant). `llama-server` raised to 4 slots (`LLM_PARALLEL=4`), a runtime setting.
- **Code**: `enrich/entities.py` (mentions with dropped spans and reasons, surface merge, node threshold),
  `finalize.py` (QID merge), `link.py` (Wikipedia search, MiniLM scoring, guards), `relations.py` (all seven
  predicates), `graph5.py` (build, validator, counts), `load5.py` (port 7690 only), `e2.py`/`e3.py`
  orchestration, `quality.py` (E4 freeze, packets, scoring), `ops/extract/embed.py`.
- **Dev decisions recorded** (`reports/enrichment-v1/link-dev.json`, `merge-dev.json`): the Wikipedia link rule
  (score at least 0.40, name must occur in the page title or lead, disambiguation and list pages excluded,
  name must match the title) accepted 8 of 120 development entities, 7 correct and 1 broader, 0 wrong; most
  specific method names have no Wikipedia page, so linking recall is low by design. Embedding-based surface
  merging never reached 90% precision on a 70-pair sample (about half at cosine 0.90 or more), so its
  threshold is 0.97 (effectively off); duplicates are handled by lemmatized keys, acronym expansion and the
  shared-QID merge.
- **Rehearsal on the first 900 papers** (ignored `data/interim/enrichment-v1-rehearsal/`): extract, link, build,
  validate (v4 layer through the frozen v4 validator, 0 errors; enrichment rules, 0 errors), and load into a
  new `neo4j-enriched` service on bolt 7690; a load to 7689 is refused. Inventory and integrity queries
  (`queries/enriched-*.cypher`) returned the expected counts and zero violations. Rehearsal counts are not results.
- **Spec deviations to record**: relations are generated from the whole abstract, not per sentence (only about a
  fifth of paper-level relations have both endpoints in one sentence; `same_sentence_papers` is stored per edge);
  the Wikipedia lead texts and abstracts stay in ignored local stores.
