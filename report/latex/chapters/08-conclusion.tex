\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This project constructed and analyzed a versioned scientific knowledge graph
for 13,617 main-track AAAI and IJCAI papers from 2021--2025. The production v4
artifact contains 47,875 nodes and 225,731 relationships across five entity
labels and ten predicates. Its evidence model distinguishes observed
bibliographic relationships from inferred topics, curated taxonomy, and derived
collaboration and topic links. The graph supports reproducible researcher,
organization, citation, community, centrality, and topic-portfolio analyses, as
well as a read-only Explorer for inspecting entities and evidence.

The results show a growing selected proceedings corpus, an 85.00\% topic-label
coverage rate, a large but incomplete researcher component, repeated
academia--industry co-publication supports, and meaningful sensitivity of bridge
rankings to identity policy. The strongest claims remain descriptive and scoped
to the frozen corpus. Affiliation coverage is 73.71\% of matched author
positions, below the 80\% project target. Historical topic agreement is limited.
The prospective edge sample's 193/200 AI-assessed result is not independently
verified course accuracy; human validation was not performed, and the methods do
not estimate recall.

The project meets the course's structural size and relationship-type requirements
in its recorded validation, and it provides a transparent basis for further
analysis. It does not establish a verified semantic-accuracy score above 90\%.
The graph should be used as a source-traceable exploratory resource, with
identity, topic, affiliation, and coverage limitations considered before any
decision is made from its rankings.
