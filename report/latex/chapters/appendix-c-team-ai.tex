\section{AI assistance and evaluation disclosure}

Repository records identify historical and prospective edge/topic judgments as
AI assessments, not human annotations. The prospective v4 audit used two
separate GPT-6 contexts, but provider builds are unavailable. The deadline
sprint used two GPT-6-Luna contexts with high reasoning effort. Both audits reuse
construction sources; evaluator independence and human validation remain
unestablished.

For this report, GPT-6.1 Sol synthesized the evidence, wrote the manuscript, and
checked the build. A delegated read-only reviewer checked selected claims and
references, but its task exposed no model or effort override and its inherited
settings are unavailable. The requested GPT-6-Luna High configuration is
therefore unverified. This remains AI editorial input, not human validation.
The team must confirm course disclosure requirements; this draft claims no
lecturer approval of AI-only evaluation.
