\section{Identity, provenance, and derivation rules}

An accepted edge is identified by (predicate, source ID, target ID), with
symmetric endpoints in canonical ID order. It records evidence IDs, method and
graph versions. Source evidence retains URLs, retrieval times, raw-response
hashes, locators and permitted excerpts.

An affiliation triple is specific to one researcher, one paper, and one
organization; aggregate Researcher--Organization edges retain paper support.
Derived evidence points to the accepted base edges and distinct core-paper IDs
that support the claim. R7 fractional weights account for unresolved byline
positions in the denominator. R8 excludes a single author's multiple
affiliations as evidence of organization collaboration. R9 and R10 thresholds
count distinct accepted works, not alternate versions or repeated records.

The five projections remain separate: directed citation rankings do not mix
with undirected collaboration. Year and country are properties. Unresolved
identities and raw source records are outside the accepted domain graph.
