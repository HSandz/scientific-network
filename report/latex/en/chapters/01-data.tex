\chapter{Data Collection and Processing}
\label{ch:data}
\section{Project scope}
This project supports research-landscape and collaboration analysis for the
course \emph{Social Media Network Analysis for Business}: find repeated
co-publication, inspect connections between groups, and compare visible
research areas. The final official roster, v10, contains \Papers{} main-track
papers from AAAI and IJCAI, 2021--2025: 9,910 AAAI and 3,707 IJCAI papers.
Edition year is a paper property. Workshops, other tracks and other venues are
excluded, so this is a bounded two-venue view rather than a census of AI research.

\section{Data sources and their roles}
Official proceedings define inclusion and printed bylines
\citep{aaaiProceedings,ijcaiProceedings}; OpenAlex enriches matched records
\citep{openalexworks}. An index record cannot silently add a paper to the roster.

\section{From official records to matched works}
The pipeline normalizes roster fields and derives DOIs from proceedings records:
AAAI uses volume, issue and record number; IJCAI uses year and paper number.
All 13,617 requested DOIs are distinct. OpenAlex is queried in cached batches,
with retries for unresolved requests. Candidates require normalized title
similarity of at least 0.90 and matching author counts; near-exact titles are
flagged. Official landing pages help distinguish duplicate-DOI candidates.
Ambiguous matches stay in review. Duplicate references collapse, and citation
targets mapping to multiple roster papers are withheld.

Acquisition recorded \MatchedWorks{} matches (99.92\%) and \UnmatchedWorks{}
review records: nine ambiguous duplicate-DOI cases and two author-count
mismatches. No record was reported as not found after retries. Unmatched papers
remain in the corpus with venue edges but no unsupported researcher identities.
All matched records have abstracts.

\textbf{Historical source caveat.} V4 acquisition lacked returned-DOI equality
checking in its singleton fallback. Seven accepted records have returned DOIs
different from the roster-derived DOIs, separate from the 11 unmatched records.
Title/byline agreement does not verify version equivalence or affiliation
transfer. The later code guard protects future acquisition; frozen v4 was not
retroactively repaired. All seven discrepancies remain unresolved.

Immutable caches retain requests, retrieval times and content hashes; evidence
locators connect claims to records. Current API responses cannot replace the
original bytes for exact reproduction.
