\section{Structural validation and evidence quality}
The recorded structural checks pass for the graph. They check unique IDs, permitted
labels and predicates, valid endpoint types, accepted status, evidence references,
and recomputed support and weights for derived relations. All ten predicates are
present. The recorded Neo4j load reconciles node and relationship counts;
six integrity queries and an idempotent repeated import also pass. These are
recorded validation results, not newly executed database checks for this manuscript.

A full-source consistency check compared the retained authorship and reference
objects for all 13,606 matched works, covering 65,469 positions and 341 cached
response hashes. It establishes agreement with the preserved source snapshot.
Structural consistency and source-byte equality cannot establish the correctness
of provider identities or affiliations.

\textbf{LLM-assisted review.} Edge and topic assessments used large language
models (LLMs); the report was reviewed using GPT-6.1-Sol.
The source-backed edge review judged 193 of 200 uniformly sampled accepted
edges supported (96.50\%; Wilson 95\% interval 92.95--98.29\%), with one
incorrect and six unverifiable judgments. Unverifiable cases count as
nonsupport. A later relation-stratified diagnostic flagged three topic-derived
links and one uncertain authorship; these findings are recorded separately
from the uniform sample and did not change the graph.

Coverage losses in Table~\ref{tab:coverage} affect what can be seen in the
networks. The graph has 199 papers without accepted authorship, and affiliation
evidence is absent for part of the official byline population. Topic coverage
also measures availability rather than correctness. On 50 historical test
papers, embedding labels had micro precision 0.6863, recall 0.3535 and F1 0.4667
against the review reference labels, compared with phrase-baseline F1 0.2301.
These results support using topic labels to explore research themes alongside
the observed publication counts and accepted-edge inventories.
