\chapter*{Project Overview}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Project Overview}
This course project turns publication records into a network for exploring who
publishes together, which organizations co-publish, how research topics vary,
and which papers receive citations within a defined collection. It covers
\Papers{} main-track papers from AAAI and IJCAI, 2021--2025. Official
proceedings define the collection; OpenAlex supplies additional metadata for
\MatchedWorks{} matched works.

The frozen production v4 graph contains \TotalNodes{} nodes and \TotalEdges{}
edges across five entity types and ten relationships. Construction combines
DOI-based paper matching, position-specific researcher identification,
paper-specific affiliation mapping, and a frozen MiniLM topic classifier.
The classifier compares titles and abstracts with topic definitions, allows
several labels per paper, and abstains on uncertain inputs. Observed publication
facts, inferred topics and derived collaboration links remain distinguishable.
Neo4j Community stores the graph; offline analysis supplies the report and the
read-only Research Explorer.

The selected corpus grows from 2,240 papers in 2021 to 4,042 in 2025, after a
2022 dip. Its researcher network has \CoauthorPairs{} distinct coauthor pairs;
20,776 of 32,189 researchers lie in the largest connected component. Organization
analysis identifies 3,533 academia--industry pair--paper supports and repeated
co-publication around several inferred topics. Multimodal learning gains share
among labeled papers in both venues. \emph{Informer} leads the within-corpus
citation ranking with 51 citing papers. These results describe the selected
publication network; missing identities and affiliations, inferred-topic errors,
and the restricted citation boundary limit wider interpretation.
